Ready for Testing · PR #436

Move Trips — Conflict Highlighting

EZRD-2059 · Testing Walkthrough

How to verify the redesigned conflict view in the Move Trips tools — salmon rows, stacked overlap lanes, the candycane merged block, the collapse/expand toggle, and the conflict reasons in the hover tooltip. Environment-agnostic: run it on whatever environment carries the PR branch.

What's under test

A front-end redesign, not a detection fix

The underlying conflict detection (overlap decoupled from the deadhead pass, stale-flag reset, trip-ends carried inline on the paginated wire) already shipped separately in EZRD-2137 (#395). What this ticket delivers is the visual conflict model. So this walkthrough is about what you see, not whether a conflict is computed.

The one rule to remember

A row fills salmon ( #ffc0c1) when any of three checks trips: overlap, insufficient deadhead (too close to the previous trip), or bell-time violation. Every salmon row shows a red "!" marker.

Only overlaps add lanes

Overlapping trips stack into lanes (row grows taller), and at 5+ concurrent they collapse into one hatched candycane block with a count badge. Deadhead and bell-time conflicts stay single-lane — they color the row and show the marker, but don't stack.

Before you start

Two places the conflict view appears

Both are reached from the Operations dropdown. Test both — they share the layout math but render through different components.

1 · Move Trips (between routes)

From the full Routes list → Operations → Move Trips. The slide-out gantt panel. Interactive — you can drag trips between routes.

2 · Move Trips to Other Routes (single route)

From a single route → Operations → Move Trips to Other Routes. Its "Preview Edits" tab is read-only (no drag) and shows the same conflict view — overlap + deadhead only, no bell-time.

Reference data

On the testqa district, known conflict examples are West Elementary (overlapping trips) and West Alternative (bell-time conflict). Any district with overlapping or tightly-spaced trips will do — the steps below don't depend on a specific tenant.

The walkthrough

Step through each behavior

Each step is an action and the expected result. Do these in the Move Trips (between routes) panel first, then repeat the relevant ones in the Preview Edits modal (Step 9).

1
Cold-open visibility (the original bug)

Open Move Trips on a district that has a known conflict. Do not hover or mouse over any row first.

Expected: conflicting rows are already salmon with a "!" marker the instant the panel renders — no mouseover / warm-up needed.
2
Overlap → stacked lanes (2–4 concurrent)

Find (or create, by dragging a trip so its time overlaps another on the same route) a row with 2–4 overlapping trips.

Expected: the row grows taller; overlapping trips stack into top-aligned lanes; non-overlapping trips share a lane; row fills salmon; "!" marker present.
3
Overlap → candycane merged block (5+ concurrent)

Build a 5+ overlap (see Constructing a 5+ overlap below), then look at that row.

Expected: the overlap renders as one hatched candycane block with a count badge (e.g. "5"). Click it → it expands into the individual stacked lanes.
4
Collapse / Expand overlaps toggle

On any row with an overlap (2+), use the blue Collapse overlaps / Expand overlaps pill in the row header.

Expected: "Collapse overlaps" folds the lanes into a candycane and flips the pill to "Expand overlaps"; "Expand overlaps" restores the individual lanes. State is per-row.
5
Deadhead conflict (too close to previous trip)

Find a route where two consecutive trips are spaced closer than the travel time between them.

Expected: row is salmon, single-lane (no stacking), "!" marker on the later trip. Hovering shows Insufficient Deadhead — need N min in the tooltip.
6
Bell-time conflict

Find a route whose drop-off/pick-up violates a school's bell time (e.g. West Alternative on testqa).

Expected: row is salmon, single-lane, "!" marker. Tooltip shows Bell Time Conflict. (A school with no bell time set must NOT be flagged — that was a false-positive we fixed.)
7
Conflict reasons in the hover tooltip

Hover a conflicting trip.

Expected: below a divider at the bottom of the tooltip, the reason(s) render — Overlapping Trips, Insufficient Deadhead, and/or Bell Time Conflict. A trip with multiple conflicts lists all of them.
8
Drag ergonomics

Drag a trip within/between routes; also click a trip without moving it; and drag the last conflicting trip off a conflicting route.

Expected: no tooltip pops mid-drag; a tiny (sub-3px) wiggle counts as a click, not a move; when the last conflicting trip leaves a route, that row's salmon clears (no stale highlight). The salmon updates live as trips move in/out of conflict.
9
Preview Edits modal (read-only parity)

From a single route → Operations → Move Trips to Other Routes, move a trip, then open the Preview Edits tab.

Expected: the same conflict view — salmon rows, stacked lanes / candycane, "!" markers, tooltip reasons — but read-only (no drag). It shows overlap + deadhead conflicts; bell-time is intentionally not shown here. Deadhead salmon should appear even though it resolves a moment after the panel opens (async).
Setup aside

Constructing a 5+ overlap (for the candycane)

Seed data rarely has five trips running at the same instant, so you'll usually need to build one. The candycane appears at 5+ concurrent trips (four or fewer just stack into lanes).

1

Pick a destination route and note the start time of one of its trips.

2

Drag trips from other routes onto that route so that five or more of them are running during the same window (overlapping in time). The easiest way is to pile several short trips that all start at roughly the same time.

3

Once peak concurrency hits 5, the stacked lanes collapse into a single hatched candycane block with a "5" (or higher) count badge. Click it to expand, use the pill to collapse again.

Boundary check

At exactly 4 concurrent trips you should see 4 stacked lanes, not a candycane. At 5, it should merge. That 4→5 boundary is the specific threshold to confirm.

Regression checks

Two things surfaced in code review

Quick sanity checks on the two fixes made during review of PR #436.

Duration formatting

In tooltips, a duration near a minute boundary must read cleanly (e.g. "1h 0m"), never "1h 60m" or "60m".

Zero-duration / co-located trips

Two trips at the same instant (same start = end) must still split into separate lanes and stay hoverable — neither one hidden behind the other.
