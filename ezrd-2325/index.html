EZRD-2325 P1 · S2 High Testing guide PR #690

Off-day trip variants must appear on the first stop selection

Spokane reported that a Monday late-start trip is missing from the “other trips” panel when assigning students, and only shows up if you repeat the selection.

?

What you're testing

When you put a student on a bus from the Routes tab, after you pick a trip and click a stop the app shows a panel headed “Choose any other trips you'd also like to assign these students to.” That panel exists because one route usually runs several day-variants — the same run on different days with different times. A router almost always wants the student on all of them.

The bug: that panel only listed trips running on the day you're currently viewing. A variant that doesn't run that day was missing entirely, so the student couldn't be added to it in the same action.

The single most important thing about this test: you must view the route on a day when the two variants disagree. On a day when both run, the panel looks correct whether or not the fix is present — you'd be testing nothing. Every step below pins the day for you.

B

Re-test after the fix deploys to UAT

This is the test to run. The fix is deployed as of 4 August 2026 — verified by checking that the merge commit for PR #690 is an ancestor of the build reported by /version, and that the served UI bundle contains the new code. Note that the branch commits alone will look absent, because the PR was squash-merged; check the merge commit. Now follow the steps in Part A and expect the result below.

Expected: the panel lists exactly one more row than it did in Part A (9 where Part A showed 8), and that extra row is the long-named variant showing RECURRENCE: Every week on Monday, Wednesday, Friday — its own days, not the route's “Every weekday”. It also shows a real TIME, DURATION and DISTANCE rather than blanks.

Then check these five things:

  • First attempt only. It must appear the first time. Needing to back out and retry is the original bug.
  • Correct recurrence on the row. If the extra row says “Every weekday” instead of Mon/Wed/Fri, that's a regression — it's showing the route's recurrence, not the trip's.
  • Repeat the selection. Cancel, redo it. Same trips, and no duplicated rows.
  • Tick it and save. You should get two success toasts, one per trip. Re-open the student and confirm the second assignment sits on the Mon/Wed/Fri schedule — not weekday.
  • Nothing else moved. The route row still shows the same split count as in Part A, the map still draws only that day's trips, and the sidebar counts are unchanged. The fix deliberately does not widen what the map shows.
Also worth one pass: a route whose trips all share one recurrence should behave exactly as it always has — same trips offered, nothing extra.

Notes & known limits

  • Not Spokane-specific. Any tenant with more than one recurrence on a route is affected. Tenants with a single recurrence per route never saw it.
  • Why the old workaround worked. Two different loads populate the trip list with different scopes; backing out and retrying could get the unfiltered one to win. That's why it looked intermittent rather than broken.
  • Degraded path is safe by design. If the extra trip list can't be loaded, the panel falls back to showing only the current day's trips — the old behaviour. It will never offer a trip whose schedule it couldn't confirm, because saving that could write the assignment against the wrong schedule.
  • Tested before shipping: the whole flow was driven by hand on testqa before and after the change, including saving and confirming both assignments landed on their own schedules.

Data in this guide was read from the UAT and production databases on 3 August 2026 and re-verified against UAT on 4 August 2026 (route, both trips and their recurrences, the shared stop, the four named students, and the day-activity of each recommended date all unchanged). The exact trip counts are still a snapshot — what matters is the difference between Part A and Part B (one extra row), not the absolute numbers. Trip and route names can be renamed by district staff — if a name here no longer matches, look for the pair by recurrence instead.
